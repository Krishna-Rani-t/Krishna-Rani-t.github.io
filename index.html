About

I’m finishing my M.Sc. in Computer Science at Leibniz Universität Hannover and work as a student assistant in the Scientific Knowledge Organization (SciKnowOrg) group at TIB – Leibniz Information Centre for Science and Technology. My work centres on LLMs, NLP and model evaluation: building the pipelines that produce benchmark data, and the tooling that scores models against it.

For my master’s thesis, supervised by Dr. Jennifer D’Souza and Prof. Dr. Sören Auer, I built HighSchoolBench, a multimodal benchmark of real Grades 9–12 exam questions, and evaluated 22 language and vision-language models on it. At TIB I help maintain OntoLearner and OntoAligner, open-source Python libraries for LLM-based ontology learning and alignment.

Before my master’s I spent more than four years at Bosch as a software engineer on automotive infotainment audio: external amplifiers, volume control and audio-source prioritisation for Mitsubishi, Nissan and Renault projects. I still bring that habit of testing, integrating and tracking down why something fails into my ML work.

Selected work

HighSchoolBench

Master’s thesis, Leibniz Universität Hannover, 2026

A multimodal benchmark of authentic multiple-choice and open-ended questions from 25 high-school assessments and competitions. Passages, equations, diagrams, charts and tables stay attached to each question, so models are tested on what students actually see.

I built the pipeline that turns exam PDFs into structured JSON (Chandra and DeepSeek-OCR, Qwen3-32B extraction, automated checks and manual review), and an evaluation tool that ran 22 models on 8,393 test questions using H100 and L40S GPUs.

  • FedLIME: federated learning with LIME

    University project

    Explains models trained across clients without moving their data. Each client computes LIME explanations locally and shares only noise-protected summaries. Federated explanations reached up to 0.96 similarity to the centralised baseline.
